Abstract This article displays an overview of the important types of cyberthreats affecting users and organizations today. It discusses how threats such as malware, ransomware, phishing, and insider attacks continue to grow as technology advances. The page displays how cybercriminals use vulnerabilities in systems, networks, and human behavior to get unauthorized access or interrupt operations. It also highlights the need of precautionary security measures, including monitoring, employee training, and strong authentication practices. Overall, the article helps readers learn more about the growing of cyber risks and why cybersecurity strategies must adapt to stay ahead of attackers.
